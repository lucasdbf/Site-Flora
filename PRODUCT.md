# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Confirmed: static HTML/CSS/JS, three.js (vendored, no build step) for the 3D viewer. Deploy target: GitHub Pages, repo `git@github.com:lucasdbf/Site-Flora.git`.

## Users
Visitors who land on the Florá site to look at the first-collection T-shirt and decide to get in touch to buy it. School demo: the real audience is the class and teacher evaluating an entrepreneurship project, but the site must work as if it were a real store.

## Product Purpose
Showcase and "sell" one white T-shirt (embroidered yellow ribbon + red outlined heart) from Florá's first collection, in support of childhood cancer. Success: a visitor understands the cause, interacts with the 3D shirt, and taps the contact button. No checkout: purchase happens by contact (size, price, shipping are "found out" via contact).

## Positioning
Florá ("a arte de florescer") is a T-shirt brand with a reason to buy: a new collection every two months, 70% of net profit donated to an NGO supporting that collection's cause. Collection 1 supports GRAACC (Grupo de Apoio ao Adolescente e à Criança com Câncer, founded in Brazil in 1991).

## Operating Context
School entrepreneurship assignment, treated as if real. Fictional business: no CNPJ/MEI, no proof of donation required. Contact is by a demo button (WhatsApp with a fictional number and a prefilled message is the proposal; not yet confirmed). Authors per slides: Maria Cristina, Maria Laura, Laura (turma 1e).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Single product, front-view PNG only (`Camiseta Branca com Laço e Coração.png`); back is undesigned.
- Interactive 3D model of the shirt, to be modelled in Blender (MCP connected) and exported for the web.
- Price, sizes, shipping: intentionally absent; revealed via contact.
- Language: Brazilian Portuguese.
- Next collections: planned every 2 months, causes not yet defined.
- Commits as `lucasdbf_dev` / `lucasfernandes1829@gmail.com`.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Florá. Tagline: "a arte de florescer".
- Visual identity comes from the logo (`rotulo_maria.pdf`): five heart-shaped petals in brown, dusty pink, tan, sage green, grey, off-white centre; black serif wordmark with a brown "á". The slide deck's yellow/burgundy look is explicitly NOT to be used.
- Mission copy from the deck (ideia inicial, como faremos isso, primeira coleção) is the source of truth for text.

## Product Principles
1. The cause is the reason to buy; the shirt is how you take part.
2. The 3D shirt is the centrepiece, and every other section serves it.
3. Only claim what the deck states (70% of net profit, GRAACC); invent no numbers, reviews or customers.
4. Demo-grade contact, but built so a real number or link replaces the placeholder.
